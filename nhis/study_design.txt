1) Research objective
To evaluate whether calcium channel blocker (CCB) initiation is associated with a higher risk of incident fracture compared with diuretic (DU) initiation among adults with hypertension.

2) Research data source
Korean National Health Insurance Service Sample Cohort Data, version 1.0.

3) Study target
Adults aged ≥20 years (`nhid_jk.AGE_GROUP > 3`) with at least one hypertension diagnosis (`I10` in `nhid_gy40_t1.SICK_SYM`, `nhid_gy20_t1.MAIN_SICK`, or `SUB_SICK`). Eligibility requires continuous insurance coverage verified by `nhid_jk.STND_Y` for every calendar year overlapping the 365-day baseline period preceding the index date. Exclude patients with any CCB or DU prescription in 2002 or within the 365-day baseline period, patients prescribed both CCB and DU on the index date, and patients with a fracture diagnosis during the baseline period. All specified requirements are implementable with the provided schema.

4) Exposure definition
Index date is the date of the first eligible CCB or DU prescription between 2003 and 2012, identified from `nhid_gy60_t1.RECU_FR_DT` where `nhid_gy60_t1.GNL_NM_CD` matches the first four characters of `Study drug mapping.gnl_nm_cd_4` (group=1: CCB, group=0: DU). Patients are classified as CCB initiators or DU initiators based on the class prescribed on the index date. Follow-up begins on the index date. Patients are excluded if both classes appear on the same index date.

5) Outcome definition
Incident fracture is defined as the first occurrence of diagnosis codes with prefixes S02, S07, S12, S22, S23, S42, S52, S62, S72, S82, or S92 in `nhid_gy40_t1.SICK_SYM` or `nhid_gy20_t1.MAIN_SICK`/`SUB_SICK` occurring on or after the index date.

6) Confounders
Baseline covariates measured within the 365-day period before the index date:
- Demographics: `nhid_jk.SEX`, `nhid_jk.AGE_GROUP`
- Socioeconomic status: `nhid_jk.CTRB_PT_TYPE_CD`
- Disability severity: `nhid_jk.DFAB_GRD_CD`
- Comorbidities: Diagnostic codes from `nhid_gy40_t1.SICK_SYM`/`nhid_gy20_t1.MAIN_SICK`/`SUB_SICK` matched to `Comorbidity mapping.code`
- Concomitant medications: Drug codes from `nhid_gy60_t1.GNL_NM_CD` matched to `Concomitant medication mapping.code`
- Healthcare utilization: Claim count in `nhid_gy20_t1` (outpatient/inpatient) within baseline.

7) Statistical strategy

Propensity score matching will be applied to reduce baseline differences between CCB and DU initiators. Propensity scores will be estimated using baseline demographic and clinical covariates, followed by 1:1 nearest-neighbor matching. Covariate balance before and after matching will be assessed using standardized mean differences.

Cox proportional hazards regression will be performed in the propensity score-matched cohort with left truncation at the index date. Time scale: days from index date to outcome, censoring, or end of available data. Proportional hazards assumption will be evaluated using Schoenfeld residuals. All baseline confounders will be modeled as time-invariant covariates. Complete-case analysis will be applied per cohort eligibility criteria.

8) Analysis method

Primary analysis: Cox model in the propensity score-matched cohort estimating hazard ratios and 95% confidence intervals for fracture risk comparing CCB initiators vs. DU initiators. Secondary analysis: Kaplan–Meier survival curves with log-rank test in the matched cohort. Censoring will occur at death (`nhid_jk.DTH_YM`), loss of insurance eligibility (`nhid_jk.STND_Y` gaps), or end of data availability. Sensitivity analyses will test robustness by varying baseline period length and excluding events within 30 days post-index.
